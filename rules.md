# 📜 Server Rules

Welcome to the community!  
To keep the server safe and fun for everyone, please follow these rules.

---

## 1. Be Respectful
Treat everyone with kindness.  
No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.

## 2. No Spamming
No repeated messages, links, or annoying pings.

## 3. Keep Channels Relevant
Post in the correct channel.  
Read channel descriptions before sending messages.

## 4. No NSFW Content
This includes text, images, videos, and links.

## 5. No Illegal Activities
Anything involving piracy, hacking, or malicious actions is not allowed.

## 6. Follow Discord TOS
All members must follow the official Discord Terms of Service.

---

## ⚠️ Punishments
Breaking rules may result in:

- Warnings  
- Timeouts  
- Kicks  
- Bans  

> Staff will choose the appropriate action depending on severity.
